1) Chunk level embedding:
-------------------------
- preceise retrieval 
- Exact table/para
- Less noise in context
- Better for focussed qns

Cons: More complexity  and higher costs


2)Page level embedding:
------------------------
- Simple
- Fewer embeddings
- Faster DB build
- Good for broad qns

Cons - less precise, no localization


metadata:
---------
- Allows to filter before searching or enriching
- Incases of document storage, store chunk type, page num and coordinates of bounding box 
as metadata.
