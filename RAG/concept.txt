Rag involves six steps across 3 phases:

A) Pre-processing:
------------------
1) Parse (chunks) - ADE major job
2) Embed (Vectors)
3) Store ( Vector DB ) - Chroma DB for local db


B) Query:
---------
1) Query and rank
2) Retrieve - Relevent chunks + metadata

C) Generate:
-------------
- Generate augmented response


RAG - Process:
--------
1) Embed: Convert qn to a Vector
2) Search: Find tok-k nearest Vectors
3) Score: Rank fetched vectors by similarity score
4) Filter: Remove vectors beneath a minimum similarity threshold
5) Visualize: Show chunks as cropped images from pdf.


- Retrieved chunk from step 4 is sent to prompt as context
