OCR (optical character recognition):
-------------------------------------
- Converts pdf or image to text
- Exctraction needs text as input.
- Without OCR, parsing tools cannot see text in scanned images.

Two steps of OCR:
1) Image processing: Preprocessing corrects tilted scans through deskewing and removes
digital noise with despeckling algos.
Deskewing -> properly aligning either horizontally or vertically incase they are tilted.
Despeckling -> Operation removes digital noise like random dots or blurr from image.
It makes the image cleaner, enhancing the clarity of textual content.

2) Text recognition: Advanced pattern matching compares image shapes against a comprehensive
library of known characters.

- OCR is good at extracitng text from clean, well formatted documents.
- OCR parses text but does not understand the info and this requires and intelligent post processing.

OCR fails for:
- Poor image quality
- Complex layouts
- Non standard texts that are handwritten
- Postprocessing is important for structure, recognition and relationships in the text.


- AI agents add a cognitive layer on top of OCR.
