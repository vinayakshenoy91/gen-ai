- Agentic document extraction
1) Vision first - Treats documents as visual objects where meaning is encoded in layout, structure 
and spatial relationships

2) Data centric : Train on the highest quality, curated data.Right data id important as right 
model architecture

3) Agentic: Delivery systems that plan, decide, act and verify outputs until the response
meets quality thresholds.
