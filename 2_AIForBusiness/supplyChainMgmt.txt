- Supply chain mgmt makes sure products are available and delivered on time.
- Coordination of all activities involved in producing and delivering a product.

- Goals of SCM: Reduce cost, meet customer demand, timely deliveries.

Elements of SCM are:

1) Demand forecasting: Predict quantity, timing and demand for a product
2) Inventory mgmt: Have enough stock without overstocking
[Reorder points, Safety stock and Economic order quantity]
