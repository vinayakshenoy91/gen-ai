Limitations to existing frameworks:
-----------------------------------
1) Reliability and trust
2) Decision making errors [Compounding errors]
3) Plan divergence and looping: diverging from the intended plan, and an inability to fix their own mistakes.

AgentQ uses below method to self correct agents:

//To search, identify failures  and find better options.
1) Monte Carlo Tree Search (MCTS)
MCTS allows agents to simulate and evaluate different paths in advance, 
which supports planning and optimal decision-making.


2) Self-critique mechanism and process supervision:
Use and LLM critique to give feedback and improve reasoning.Self-critique mechanisms leverage feedback to 
help agents analyze their actions and refine their reasoning for continuous improvement.
RLHF -> Refine Preferences and learns from boht successful and failed trajectories.

//To learn and understand 
3) Direct Preference Optoimisation (DPO)
- Directly optimizing LLM based on Preferences
Learns from both successful and failed trajectories.

- DPO enables agents to learn efficiently by directly using preference data, 
skipping the step of constructing a separate reward model.


Note: Exploitation leverages current knowledge for predictable outcomes, 
while exploration involves trying new possibilities to potentially find better results.
