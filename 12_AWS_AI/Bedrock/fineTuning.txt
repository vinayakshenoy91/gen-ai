How to train?
- For text based models provide prompts and completions and put this in S3
- Tell bedrock to make the custom model from this foundation model using the data that is given
- Prompts and link to images in S3 for image model training
- Use VPC with private link for sensitive training data.
- Continued pre-training - Like finetuning, but unlabelled data. Just feed it with text 
to familiarize the model with your own business documents.
Include extra data into the model so that you do not have to include it in prompt.

Approach used for fine tuning: LORA (Low rank adaption)
-------------------------------------------------------
- We dont update the entire model, just slap some "low rank matrices" to the attention weights
and train those."Low rank" refers to the complexity of the underlying matrices in the model.

- At inference, these fine-tuned weights get added into the base model.

- Very effecient for storage, training and inference.
